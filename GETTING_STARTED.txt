PUTT TOGETHER {{VERSION}} - WINDOWS BETA

INSTALL

Extract the entire ZIP into a writable folder and run PuttTogether.exe.
Keep all extracted files together. You do not need Godot or a player account.
The beta uses a keyboard and mouse and runs on 64-bit Windows.

YOUR FIRST ROUND

Open Explore parks and choose Pocket Links for a short three-hole course.
Choose Play a round to start. Optional bots can join a solo round.
Use experimental bot AI is off by default; the host can enable it before starting.

Press 1 for Putter and F to find your ball. Face roughly along your shot line.
Hold LEFT MOUSE, move RIGHT to draw back, then LEFT to swing through.
Keep the button held during the stroke. Start with a small, slow movement.
Release it to leave the stance.

To aim, keep left mouse held and hold RIGHT MOUSE as well. Move left/right
to adjust the line, then release right mouse before making your stroke.
Q/E fine-tunes aim. Whenever Putter is equipped, the wheel or [ / ] adjusts
shot power; Shift+wheel makes finer changes. No mouse buttons need to be held.
Power locks when the club starts moving; release left mouse to adjust again.

Settings lets you change Easy/Advanced putting, stroke direction, handedness
and sensitivity. Changing handedness does not change the mouse stroke axis.
The distance line is a guide: pace, contact and the course still affect the shot.

After holing out, press N to advance or finish your round. With Putter equipped,
Tab releases the cursor and focuses Next hole / Finish round; click or press Enter.
When everyone has finished, open the scorecard and choose Back to building.

DEFAULT CONTROLS

Shift / Space  Sprint / jump
1 / 2 / 3      Putter / Select / Pieces
4 / 5 / 6      Terrain / Draw / Water
B              Piece library
Tab            Tool options / next-hole cursor controls after holing out
F1             Full controls reference and keyboard remapping
F2             Optional debug overlays
F              Find your ball
N              Next hole / finish round after holing out
E              Place your ball at a tee / push a drawbridge button
P              Place a practice ball between rounds
Backspace      Retry your lie (a penalty applies in scored rounds)
H              Hole list
G              Contextual guides
C / V          Toggle connections / world-grid snapping while building
Shift+V        Show or hide the placement grid
Alt+Enter      Toggle fullscreen
Ctrl+S         Save park
Ctrl+Z         Undo your edit
Escape         Cancel the current action or release the mouse

BUILDING

Create a park starts an empty garden or arcade. B opens the piece library.
Click to place a piece; right mouse cancels the preview. Q/E selects the
highlighted adjustment, and the wheel changes it. Hold Shift for finer steps.
Supported dimensions each have their own adjustment and numeric Tab field.
Elevation moves the whole piece vertically. Tab also has snapping and other settings.
Hold Shift while placing to temporarily suspend connections and grid snapping.
The visible grid can stay on even when snapping is off.

Use 2 to select a piece; Shift-click adds or removes pieces from a group.
M moves a selection, Ctrl+D copies it, and Ctrl+B opens blueprints.
Use H, then Add hole, to place its tee and cup. Both are needed for a round.

CUSTOMIZE

Choose Customize from the menu. Pick a ball color and pattern, then choose a
putter style and finish. Apply saves your choices; Cancel keeps your old set.
The starter collection is unlocked and styles do not change putting physics.

WILLOW ISLAND

Willowbend's last hole crosses a moat. Look at either drawbridge button and
press E. The bridge lowers, holds for its displayed time, then rises again.
Press again to extend the timer. Builders can change its size and open duration.

SAVE YOUR PARK

Ctrl+S saves; Save as keeps a separate copy. Included courses can be edited
and saved as your own parks. Recent unsaved work may be available through
Explore parks > Recovery. Recover it, then Save to keep it.

Your parks, settings and blueprints are in %APPDATA%\PuttTogether, outside
this game folder. Recovery copies preserve courses, not running matches.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS

Everyone should use the same version. Open a park, then choose Play with
friends > Host this park. Share the host's connection address. The host must
keep the game open. See PLAY_WITH_FRIENDS.md for LAN and internet setup.

UPDATES AND HELP

The game checks for updates on startup, with a cooldown between checks.
You can also choose Check for Updates from the main menu. Update and Restart
installs a downloaded build when you are ready; Later lets you keep playing.
Updating the host ends the shared session. Saved parks and settings stay put.

Player guides, patch notes and connection help:
https://github.com/RyanFDev/putt-together-releases

If something goes wrong, include the version, what you were doing and whether
you were hosting or joining. The repository has a troubleshooting guide and
an issue tracker. A screenshot or short recording is useful too.
