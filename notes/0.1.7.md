# Putt Together 0.1.7

This update makes parks easier to find and open, gives you clearer building
choices, and keeps Marble runs moving.

## Parks and settings

- Browse personal and premade parks in groups, save favorites with the heart
  buttons, and combine category, search, venue and sorting controls.
- Open parks faster, with a loading screen while the course becomes ready.
  Fix a false unsaved-changes warning during course loading.
- Find options in Display, Putting, Comfort and Park settings tabs. Apply a
  window resolution, set the park's time of day, pause daylight or adjust day length.
  Pausing daylight leaves balls and machinery running.

## Building and playing

- Keep Marble runs rolling with a **Frictionless** setting, enabled by default
  on new pieces and existing pieces without an override. Switch it off in the
  piece's effect settings for ordinary rolling resistance.
- Add **Downhill Funnel Gentle** and **Downhill Funnel Steep** pieces. Improve
  Chain Lift exits and fix backward-return contacts along lift guides.
- Identify library pieces with dimensions, resize indicators and size/drop
  badges. Related variants now stay together in the default order.
- Press **N** after holing out to advance or finish a round. With Putter equipped,
  **Tab** releases the cursor and focuses **Next hole / Finish round**; click or
  press **Enter**. The shortcuts follow your remapped controls.

## Updates

- Read formatted patch notes and expandable release history inside the game,
  with update controls kept visible while you scroll.

Everyone in a shared park should update to the same version. Saved parks,
blueprints and personal settings remain separate from the installation.

See the [controls guide](https://github.com/RyanFDev/putt-together-releases/blob/main/CONTROLS.md)
and [getting started](https://github.com/RyanFDev/putt-together-releases/blob/main/GETTING_STARTED.md)
for the current controls and menu paths.
