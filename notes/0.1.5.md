# Putt Together 0.1.5

Fresh course layouts, a new signature island hole, and more ways to make your golfer's equipment your own.

- All eleven included courses have refreshed paths, seating areas and prop placement. Outdoor greens have clearer boundaries and gentler surrounding hills; indoor courses have connected spectator paths and lounges.
- Planted garden groups fill the spaces between outdoor holes. Six new placeable landscaping pieces include river and split boulders, a flat stone, mossy rocks, ferns and a flowering shrub.
- Willowbend Ramble now has a creek with playing and walking bridges. Its final Willow Island hole has a water moat and a manual, timed drawbridge. Water uses the existing one-stroke penalty.
- The drawbridge is a reusable mechanical piece. Adjust its deck width, gate height and span length, and set how long it stays open. Press E at either button to lower it or extend its timer.
- Customize your equipment: eight ball colors, four ball patterns, three putter styles and four putter finishes. The starter collection is unlocked. Choices save locally and appear in multiplayer; they do not change putting physics. Progression rewards are not implemented yet.
- C toggles connections, V toggles grid snapping, and Shift+V shows the placement grid. Hold Shift for temporary free placement. These shortcuts can be remapped in F1 Controls.
- Path entrances can fit the edge of a curb and rise into a short access ramp.
- The render fills different window shapes, with responsive menus and HUD. Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen; Settings offers borderless and exclusive options.
- Fixed floating furniture details, including the neon bench trim, and a duplicate course-open click that could show an incorrect unsaved-changes prompt.

Open the included courses from Explore parks to see the new layouts. Your saved copies remain your own designs.
