## Putt Together 0.1.4

A maintenance release of the Windows beta. Courses, controls and gameplay are
unchanged from 0.1.3.

### Download and play

Download **PuttTogether-0.1.4-windows-x86_64.zip** below, extract the whole ZIP
into a writable folder, and run **PuttTogether.exe**. Keep all the files together.
You need 64-bit Windows, a keyboard and a mouse. Godot and a player account
aren't required. The beta executable is unsigned.

Already on 0.1.3? Choose **Check for Updates → Update and Restart** in the game.
Saved parks, settings and blueprints stay in place. Everyone in a shared park
should use the same version; updating the host ends the current session.

### First time here?

Open **Explore parks → Pocket Links** for three holes, or **Create a park** to
build your own. Press **F1** for controls. To putt with the default settings,
hold left mouse, move right to draw back, then left to swing through.

The beta includes outdoor gardens, indoor neon courses, terrain and construction
tools, solo rounds, player-hosted multiplayer and optional bots. Internet play
needs port forwarding or a tunnel; there are no platform invites or matchmaking
yet.
