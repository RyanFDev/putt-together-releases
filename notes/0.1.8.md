# Putt Together 0.1.8

This update adds experimental bot AI, a neon arcade course and more course-building
tools, alongside improvements to putting controls.

## Courses and building

- Play **Putt 'R Cade**, a nine-hole neon arcade course with new signs, wall
  graphics and surrounding scenery.
- Place **Message Signs** with your own text: walk up, look at the board and
  press **E** to edit.
- Adjust each supported piece dimension directly with **Q/E** and the wheel,
  or enter exact values in **Tab** options. Hold **Shift** for finer wheel steps.
- Add optional playable boxes and out-of-bounds regions to individual holes.
  Free practice placement away from a tee is independent of hole boundaries.
- Pair portal entrances and exits independently of holes: equip **Select** and
  press **E** on each endpoint.
- Build branching bot routes with editable checkpoint regions. Keep the default
  **Pass through region**, or choose **Stop in region** to ask a bot to come to
  rest inside. Guides are available in the included Putt 'R Cade, Pocket Links
  and Foxglove Fold courses.

## Putting and shared play

- Adjust shot power whenever Putter is equipped with the **wheel** or **[ / ]**,
  with brief on-screen feedback. Power locks when the club starts moving;
  release left mouse to leave the stance and adjust again.
- Improve stroke consistency across window sizes and fast mouse movements.
  Cancel unfinished strokes after a long input stall to prevent unintended
  powerful shots.
- Start from four numbered tee positions and improve putter contact when balls
  are close together.
- Match boost-pad activation to the full visible pad.

## Bots and display

- Try **Use experimental bot AI** in round setup. It is off by default; the host
  chooses it before starting the round.
- Improve experimental bot planning around ramps, moving obstacles, portals
  and lifts. Pause new bot planning while a human aims or strokes, and resume
  paused experimental bots when the host advances to the next hole.
- Open **F2** for optional local overlays showing boundaries, prop zones and bot
  planning targets. All overlays start off.
- Known issue: experimental bots can still cause stutter and struggle on complex
  holes, especially with several bots playing.

Everyone in a shared park should use the same version. Reopen included courses
to get their updated layouts; personal saved copies remain unchanged.

See the [controls guide](https://github.com/RyanFDev/putt-together-releases/blob/main/CONTROLS.md)
for the current controls and building tools.
