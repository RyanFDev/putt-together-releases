## Putt Together 0.1.3

The first public Windows beta. Build a course, play a round and change anything
that didn't work quite how you planned.

### In this build

- Included garden and indoor neon courses, with editable layouts.
- Track pieces, modular buildings, terrain shaping and painting, and moving
  obstacles for your own parks.
- Physical mouse putting with Easy and Advanced controls, adjustable stroke
  direction and an aiming view.
- Solo play, player-hosted groups of up to four participants and optional bots.
- Saved parks, reusable blueprints and recovery copies for recent course edits.
- An in-game updater: check for a release, download it and restart when ready.
  Updates keep the previous installation and preserve saved parks and settings.

### Download and play

Download **PuttTogether-0.1.3-windows-x86_64.zip**, extract the whole archive
into a writable folder, and run **PuttTogether.exe**. Keep all the files together.
This is an unsigned 64-bit Windows build for keyboard and mouse. You don't need
Godot or a player account.

For your first round, open **Explore parks → Pocket Links**. Press **F1** for
controls. With the default putting settings, hold left mouse, move right to
draw back, then left to swing through.

Players on older development exports need a complete release download once to
get the updater. Internet multiplayer needs port forwarding or a tunnel, and
the host must stay in the game. Platform invitations, matchmaking and host
migration aren't available yet.
